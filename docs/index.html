What the server does

  • Sends an SMS or email to your trusted contacts when SOS fires
  • Stores contacts and incident reports in a database
  • Creates a live-location link your contacts can open in a browser
  • Keeps an alert history you can review and resolve

Shake-to-SOS still runs in your browser — a server can't read your phone's motion sensor.
